A little about us

Calling eachother should be simple.

At Northstar Office Systems, communication is at the heart of everything we do. Our teams use MicroSIP to stay connected throughout the workday, whether they're coordinating installations, helping clients, or checking in with colleagues across departments.

Every employee has an internal extension, making it easy to reach the right person without interrupting the entire office. For the best experience, make sure MicroSIP is running and your extension is registered before placing a call.
